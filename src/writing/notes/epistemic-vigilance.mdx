I read the phrase _epistemic vigilance_ in Jane Friedman’s piece, [AI and Libraries: Why Librarians May Become Arbiters of Reality](https://janefriedman.com/ai-and-libraries-why-theyre-like-canaries-in-the-coal-mine/). It’s a fancy but precise way to describe something I’ve been thinking about for a while: AI hallucinations and slop introduce a tax on our learning—before believing what we read, we must judge where that knowledge came from and whether it’s credible, accurate, true.

Jane’s usage:

> ...what’s needed isn’t more instruction but something closer to a wellness intervention: relief from constant epistemic vigilance.
